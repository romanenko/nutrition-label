How the grade works

Vlada’s scale: A = 0 categories, B = 1, C = 2, D = 3, F = 4. Each category counts once across visited pages; per-page averages do not change its weight.

    The grade is an estimate from observable signals. Ads, recommendation wording and notification requests do not verify the business model, ranking algorithm or notification persistence. Other label criteria do not add penalties. Partial grades can worsen as unknown categories are assessed; an unresolved scan cannot earn A.

    Based on Vlada Bortnik’s four-category proposal.
